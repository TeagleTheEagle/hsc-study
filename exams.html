How to get the most from a past paper
  1. Print it or open it on a second screen, and do it on paper under your real exam time.
  2. Mark it yourself with the marking guidelines. Read the criteria for the band above your mark.
  3. Rewrite your weakest answer using what the guidelines ask for, then move on.
