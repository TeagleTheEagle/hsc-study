Bands for Days

Privacy and terms

Last updated 31 October 2026. This covers the website bandsfordays.com and the Bands for Days app for iPhone and iPad.

What Bands for Days is

Bands for Days is a free HSC study site made by a student in Sydney: flashcards, spaced reviews, past papers and AI marking that estimates a mark against the published marking guidelines. The AI marks are estimates to help you improve. They are not official NESA marks and are not connected to NESA or your school.

There is no advertising, no analytics tracker and no selling of data. Nothing is shared with your school. The class leaderboard shows your nickname, XP, level and streak to signed-in students in the same class code, and the general leaderboard shows the same to any signed-in student. Pick a nickname you are happy with.

Who processes the data

Cloudflare (serves the site and the marking service), Supabase (accounts and progress, Sydney region), Anthropic (the AI model that marks answers; answers are sent under Anthropic's API terms, which do not use API data to train models), and Stripe (payments on the website only). Each only receives what it needs for its job.

Your choices

  • Use it without an account. Everything except sync, the leaderboard and AI marking works signed out, and progress stays on your device.
  • Delete your account at any time: sign in, open the account panel at the top of the app, and choose Delete account. That removes your account, synced progress, answers, feedback and leaderboard row straight away, and cancels any Plus subscription. Progress saved on the device itself is cleared by clearing the site's data in your browser or deleting the app.
  • Export your marks report from Progress as text at any time.
  • Questions or a deletion request by email: support@bandsfordays.com.

Age

Bands for Days is for senior high school students. If you are under 16, check with a parent or carer before creating an account. Buying Plus on the website needs a cardholder who is allowed to pay for it.

Plus

Plus is an optional subscription bought on the website. It gives more AI marks a month and nothing else is locked behind it. Plus bought on the website also works in the app. The app itself sells nothing. Cancel at any time under Manage billing on the website; Plus stays on until the end of the period already paid for. Refunds for the current period are given on request if you have used fewer than 10 marks in it.
