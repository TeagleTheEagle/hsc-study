How past-paper marking works

Choose a questionMultiple choice marks itself. Short answers, the business report and the essays each get a text box.
Press MarkYour answer is checked against that question's official marking criteria, the sample answer and what the real markers said.
Read the feedbackA mark out of the total, the criteria row you landed in, what worked, what to add for the next row, and a full-mark example.
Download the originalsEvery exam paper, marking guideline, exemplar response, essay guide and case study is one click away under Past papers. The HSC papers come from the official NESA page.

AI marking on this site runs with your own Anthropic API key (kept in your browser), or you can open the Claude version, which has marking, account sync and the leaderboard built in. Self-marking with the criteria is always available.
